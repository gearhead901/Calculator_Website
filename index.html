Calc Workshop

Engineering calculators for robotics builds. These are prototypes, and the newest version is first.

Prototype v7

Latest18 calculators

Adds a Bolted Joint calculator in a new Fasteners group: bolt and member stiffness, preload and torque, yield, separation and fatigue safety factors (Shigley ch. 8), checked against the book's worked examples. Includes a thread stripping check (Machinery's Handbook): required thread engagement, which part fails first, and a good-practice engagement guide.
